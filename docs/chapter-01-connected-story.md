## Tala needs help, too

On reaching Rootwater Works, Tala notices that her copied low trail predates the surge. Rae checks the high-water line with her. She dates the correction, keeps the original mark, and admits that she can ask another person to check. On the drained return trail, they compare the two marks. Her People entry acknowledges their shared work. Earlier saves can hear the lesson through **Tala → Her map** after visiting the works; the game does not claim the player already experienced it.

## Two personal choices

When Rae first accepts Tala's help and when they correct the map, choose a playful, cautious or thoughtful reply. Each has three authored lines, rejoins the same story and earns a later callback. Tap a response or use **1 / 2 / 3** in the browser. These are ways of speaking, not relationship scores. Every answer retains the same clues, routes and ending.

The choice menu supports larger text, scrolls on short displays, links to History and can be paused. Android Back/Escape pauses it without choosing. Returning from pause/settings/history restores the pending prompt. A committed response saves immediately and cannot be replaced by a second quick tap. If an unfinished introduction restarts after loading, the chosen exchange is reconstructed without asking again. History includes only reached lines from the selected branch.

## The quiet after the bell

After the ringing conversation, a skippable 2.8-second view shows the recovering village without the dialogue panel. The frame takes in the homes and bell arch. The basket vignette begins nearby, and music softens. Still backgrounds keep their comfort setting.

Rae and Tala then watch together, share Minda's packet, talk about ordinary life and make room for Rae to miss Lola. The exchange remembers the selected replies and completed map lesson. Tala uses listening gestures. Spoken lines remain player-paced.

The bell remains durably rung throughout. Skipping the view starts the conversation; it does not mark the emotional scene complete. Backgrounding resolves the shot safely to a paused conversation. An unfinished conversation resumes near the arch after reloading. Finishing it records `afterBellSeen`; it does not ring again on a later load. Completed older journeys can revisit the arch to see it, or choose replay's **A village recovering**. The bridge and ending stay accessible independently.

## Returning to a journey

Continue shows **Last time, in my notebook**, a short, dismissible reminder based on completed progress. It appears when exploration is available, stays out of conversations and menus, and dismisses after twelve visible seconds. Fresh journeys and newly selected replay checkpoints do not show it. The reminder uses the selected save slot and does not change progress or history. The existing objective supplies the current direction.
