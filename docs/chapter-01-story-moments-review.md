## Remembering calamansi at mangMemory

**Rae Jacob:** Were you here before the houses?

**Mang Uban:** Before those houses. Not before people. Someone once sat here with a small tree and a very leaky water jar.

**Tala:** Who was it?

**Mang Uban:** A person who knew they would never sit in its full shade. They planted it anyway.

**Rae Jacob:** Lola says the calamansi was small when she was small.

**Mang Uban:** Then she has watched something grow slowly. Listen when she tells you how.

**Rae Jacob:** Do you ever forget something important?

**Mang Uban:** Yes. A voice becomes difficult to remember. A face loses its edges. That is why I tell the small stories while I can.

**Rae Jacob:** I will remember the leaky jar.

**Mang Uban:** Then it has traveled farther than its owner ever carried it.

**Rae Jacob:** When I get home, I’ll ask.

**Rae Jacob:** Lola has a calamansi tree from her great-grandmother. She can tell the whole story while she picks one leaf.

**Mang Uban:** And now?

**Rae Jacob:** Now I keep remembering the middle parts.
