# Chapter 1 — Device Check

Status: ready for user testing. These steps have not been marked as passed on a physical phone.

Open the running project in Expo Go, reload it and hold the phone in landscape. Use **Revisit Chapter 1** to review scenes in a separate replay slot. **Continue your journey** returns to the main save. Sound/comfort choices persist separately.

## Connected story additions

1. Replay **First steps in Silong**, meet Tala and reach her offer to walk together. Try a different response on three replays. Watch for its immediate exchange and a different callback after the bell. Confirm every response reaches the same questions and paths. On short landscape displays with Larger text, scroll the menu to all three replies and History.
2. Pause at an unanswered prompt using its pause button or Android Back/Escape. Resume; it should still need a reply. Open History and return; it should not choose or advance. Background the app before and after choosing. After reloading, the selected exchange should survive without a second prompt. Unselected branches should not appear in History.
3. Replay **The waterworks puzzle**. Notice the old map and high-water line. Choose a response, complete the lesson, and later return along the drained low trail. Tala should acknowledge the second dated mark. Older saves can use her map question to see this story.
4. In a yard replay inspect the clothesline, rain jar, herb bed, basin, sungka board and calamansi. Continue to their later callbacks at the drying rack, leaf cup, splitter, branch, string retrieval, Tala's map and Mang's memory topic. Repeat without those discoveries: every required clue and puzzle step should still work.
5. Ring the bell or replay **A village recovering**. Check the short unobstructed village view, Skip, quieter music, basket activity and the conversation afterward. Background during the shot and during the conversation. Continue should recover safely; finishing it should prevent an automatic repeat. Compare Still backgrounds and Direct/Gentle camera settings.
6. Continue an existing main/replay save at several puzzle stages. Read and dismiss the notebook reminder; check that it describes earned events and leaves the correct objective available. It should hide during dialogue/menus and expire after twelve visible seconds. Fresh journeys and new replay checkpoints should not show it.

## Movement and atmosphere review

1. Select **A new afternoon** in replay. Test tiny joystick movements beside the porch and small turns near diagonals. Target brackets and the action label should agree. Walls should prevent talking through them; Tala should catch up around obstacles without taking every nearby interaction.
2. Watch Rae's dust-brushing and listening, Tala's folded map near water and Minda/resident work gestures. Compare all four maps' lighting. Switch **Background motion** to **Still**: decorative shadows, motes, haze and the recovery vignette should freeze while useful current/puzzle feedback stays visible.
3. Open **History** partway through Lola's speech. Search a word already read, then close it with its exit or Android Back. The current line should be unchanged; later lines should be absent. Try **Dialogue size → Larger**, scroll a long speech on the shortest landscape display, then use **Continue**. Close and reopen the app and check that history and reading preferences persist.
4. Replay **A village recovering**. Look for open shutters, neighbors, the basket carrier, household items, cooking plume and existing repair changes. Visit Mang for his gift and revisit residents. Still background mode should keep the repairs visible without the walking vignette.
5. From replay's pause menu enable **Show frame pacing**, resume, and walk through each map. Note repeatable long intervals or visible stutters, device/OS and background-motion setting. The meter measures JavaScript callback cadence, not GPU FPS. It should hide in menus and never appear in the main journey.
6. Open scene choices and use Back/Escape without selecting one: return to the title or paused conversation. Switch replay scenes, continue the replay after reloading, and then **Return to my saved journey**. Main map, items, discoveries and history should be intact. Repeat with app backgrounding during a water change, retrieval and root reveal.

2. Inspect the lamp shelf, noticeboard, drying rack, seed trays, leaf cup, listening stone, tool roll, gauge and reed chimes. Each should add one notebook memory. Walk around their feet; no new object should block the main route or require an optional story to advance.

Try Steady view in Sound & comfort: the extra pan/shake should stop while water feedback stays visible. A blocked or repaired lever inspection should not trigger the beat. Pause during it, then resume; the lever stays changed and the camera effect should not replay.

Reopen the save and revisit him: the item should remain, and the gift should not duplicate. If you interrupt an unfinished offer, return and talk again to receive it.

Try Muning, the calamansi, herbs, sungka board and rain jar. Each should add one notebook entry without advancing the crossing.

## Five-minute comfort and audio review

1. Open the speaker button on the title. Try Music and Sounds/ambience Off, Soft, and On. With sounds on, tap **Preview the waybell**: it should ring once, finish naturally, and remain quiet when closing the menu. Music stays paused in settings. On iPhone, disable silent mode when checking audible playback.
2. Enter the game. Move the joystick a little, then farther: speed should increase smoothly. Release near a wall; footsteps should stop with Rae. Try Gentle and Direct camera follow. Check that the location panel, objective and thumb controls leave the character readable on your screen.
3. Pause, open notes, open settings, and background the app. Game audio should stop. The preview may play one requested bell in settings. Resume: the current area theme should return without replaying a recovered bell or completion reward. Try a browser preview too if available; if audio is blocked, tap Preview to enable it.
4. Set music off while keeping sounds on, then reverse those choices. Confirm that each setting controls its own layer. Close and reopen the game; the choices and journey should both survive.

## Character and story review

1. Watch Rae face and walk in all four directions. Check his six-frame stride, flip-flops, blinking and idle motion. Stand near Lola to see her wave; watch Tala's map and Minda's tools. Dialogue should change the speaking portrait's expression and mouth without obscuring the world actors.
2. On the shortest landscape screen available, read a long speech and scroll its text. Try every topic in a character menu, then use its top exit. Background during a personal conversation; resume and finish it. A visited check should survive reopening the game.
3. Open notes → People. Rae and Lola are known from the start; Tala, Minda and Mang appear after meeting them. Check their biographies, relationship notes and source links. After restoring the waybell, the relationship notes should acknowledge the change.
4. Revisit available NPCs from a completed save. Their optional stories should not undo repairs or require solving the puzzle again. Use replay's opening checkpoints to review first introductions safely. Report any line that feels repetitive, out of character or unclear about folklore versus our invented world.

## Waterworks and ending review

1. From the grove, follow the eastern maintenance trail. In the waterworks, walk south around the bypass via the small plank walkway, then north along the middle bank. Read the diagram and try the lever before clearing the branch: the safety response should explain what is blocked.
2. Push the branch into its recess and divert west. Watch the wheel stop and the west current appear. Inspect the bell, cancel tool selection once, then select spare kite string and let retrieval finish. Restore water east. The wheel should turn smoothly, the approach should drain, and the crooked clanging should remain silent.
3. Take the southwest low trail to Minda. Return the bell, help hang it at the arch, and ring it. Watch for the swinging bell, named marker, steady lamps, open work barrier and Minda at the arch. Repeated NPC conversations should acknowledge the completed work.
4. Return through the open maintenance path, cross the footbridge, and inspect the pale seal. Let the reveal finish. The hoofbeats should accompany their caption, and the completion cue should play once. Continue exploring, then reopen the saved game; the earned crossing stays open.

When reporting an issue, include the device/OS, the map and action, the selected sound/camera settings, what happened, and what you expected. For audio, say whether speakers or headphones were used. A screenshot or short recording is helpful when available.
