## mangGift

**Mang Uban:** Before you take the bamboo road, little traveler. Hold out your hand.

**Rae Jacob:** For a snack? Tala said a folded leaf was practically a speech.

**Mang Uban:** Then this will be an unnecessarily long conversation.

*A giant hand lowers a little wooden case. Rae needs only his thumb to lift its lid.*

**Mang Uban:** A listening seed. The case is from a fallen branch. No living tree was asked to lose a piece.

**Rae Jacob:** It is warm. Is something alive in there?

**Mang Uban:** Something waiting. Keep it dry. Do not bury it simply because someone calls it a seed.

**Tala:** What will it do?

**Mang Uban:** In a place where old roots remember, it may answer. I cannot tell you which place. Mine has never asked it the right question.

**Rae Jacob:** So it will not open the next door for us?

**Mang Uban:** Not every gift belongs to the next door. You listened before blaming a shadow, and mended more than a noisy wheel. I trust you to notice when its time comes.

*He tucks the closed case beside his spare kite string.*

**Rae Jacob:** Lola says to keep the patient part. I think I am collecting patient things.

**Mang Uban:** A useful collection. And Rae—thank you. For the quiet, and for asking.

**Rae Jacob:** I will look after it. I promise.
