# Rainlit Road map artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The inspected `assets/maps/bamboo-verge-v2.png` served as a style reference. Source images were copied into this workspace and resized to 1280 × 960 for the live maps. Native SVG layers add signs, characters, bark grain, the repair, the side rill and its current, weather, and the forming seal.

Final live assets:
- `assets/maps/rainlit-road-v1.png`
- `assets/maps/riverside-hamlet-v1.png`
- `assets/maps/quiet-spring-v1.png`

The original outputs remain as matching `*-source.png` files. Geometry was fitted to the finished artwork and reviewed in `artifacts/navigation-*-review.png`. Prompt coordinates are composition requests, not collision definitions.
